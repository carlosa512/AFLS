Jamison Reed, 32 and in his eleventh season, ran for two touchdowns and caught a third as the Voyageurs beat the 7–1 Squall 35–14.

Seattle lost quarterback Jordan Patterson to a concussion in the second quarter. Rookie Cameron Cox threw for 110 yards in relief, the Squall gave the ball away three times, and Minnesota closed with 21 points in the fourth.

He’s 32 and he was our best player in the fourth quarter.Tony Barrett, Voyageurs head coach

In Week 4 the Gazette had Minnesota third on offense and 31st on defense. This time the defense had three sacks and three takeaways. The Voyageurs are 5–4 and half a game behind Green Bay, who lost by 31 in Las Vegas.

Minnesota go to San Francisco next. Seattle, still a game clear in the Frontier West, host Green Bay.
