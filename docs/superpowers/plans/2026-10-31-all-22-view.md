# All-22 and Highlight Levels Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or
> superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add highlight levels (Key, Extended, Full) to both match views, and a second, top-down view (All-22) that
stages all 22 players as numbered dots to match what the engine decided.

**Architecture:** The engine writes down who did what on each play and who was on the field, adding no random draws.
A pure stager in `src/ui/stage/` turns one play into tracks for 22 dots and the ball. Players in the play follow
scripts that hit the engine's spots; everyone else steers by role. An SVG component plays the tracks back.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, React 18, SVG, Vitest; the engine's `Rng` (`src/engine/rng.ts`) for seeded staging.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-all-22-view-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- The match engine's results must not move: no new draws on any stream. Record only what the engine already computes.
- No `Math.random` anywhere in this work. Staging seeds the engine's `Rng` with
  `${league.seed}:${game.season}:${game.id}:stage:${index}`.
- `League` stays plain JSON. The new fields are optional; bump `SAVE_VERSION` (70 today) and add a `migrate.ts` step.
- Screens follow `docs/UI.md`: a player's name only through `PlayerLink`.
- Per-device picks go in `localStorage` with keys in `theme.tsx`'s style (`afls-viewer-highlights`,
  `afls-viewer-view`), every read and write wrapped in try/catch, never in the save.
- All-22 speeds are 1× and 2× only. The broadcast view keeps its speeds (replay 1/2/4, live 1/2/4/8).
- Highlight levels default to Full. All-22 is hidden for games without `result.field`.
- Each PR bumps the last number of `package.json`'s version (whatever main has at the time) and adds a
  `src/ui/changelog.ts` entry in a player's words (`docs/VERSIONING.md`). None finishes an MVP item.
- Each part below is its own PR off main. After a part merges, restart `claude/kind-tesla-l4kvva` from main for the
  next one.
- Local checks: `npm run typecheck`, the tests you touched (`npx vitest run <file>`), `npm run format` before each
  commit. Part 2 also runs `npm run sim -- 10`. Never run `npm run check`; CI runs the plan.

## Review Focus

- **Switching views mid-play in a live game:** the switch must keep the same play, score and clock. It must never
  restart the play from the snap or skip one (Task 11).
- **Odd log entries:** penalties, two-point tries, untimed downs, overtime and announcements with no `kind`. Every
  entry must stage, or be passed over, without throwing (Task 9: stage every entry of 40 games).
- **Mid-game injuries:** a player who left the game must never appear after his change point. A spelling back or a
  rotation rusher in the play gets swapped in for the man at his spot (Task 6).
- **Old and pruned games:** no All-22 switch for them. Highlight levels still work, and a scoring-only log still shows
  "Replay not available" (Tasks 2 and 11).
- **`localStorage` that throws** (private mode, blocked storage): the picks fall back to their defaults and the
  viewer still works (Task 2).

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:

  - `HIGHLIGHT_LEVELS: readonly HighlightLevel[]` (`['key', 'extended', 'full']`)
  - `highlightLevel(plays: readonly PlayLogEntry[], index: number, homeId: number): HighlightLevel`
  - `shows(plays: readonly PlayLogEntry[], index: number, homeId: number, pick: HighlightLevel): boolean`
  - `nextShown(plays: readonly PlayLogEntry[], from: number, homeId: number, pick: HighlightLevel, dir: 1 | -1): number`:
    the first index after (or before) `from` that `shows`, or -1 if there is none.

The rules, from the spec, with one calibration (Extended's yardage bar is 15+, not 10+: a probe of 40 games put 10+
at 84 plays a game, over the 60–80 range, and 15+ at 77):

- **The gain** is `(to - from) * dir`, where `dir` is 1 when `offense === homeId`, else -1. It counts only when
  `down > 0`.
- **The margin** is the scores *before* the snap: the previous entry's `homeScore - awayScore`, or 0–0 for the first
  entry.
- **Key:**
  - the text matches `/TOUCHDOWN|FUMBLE|BLOCKED|SAFETY/`;
  - `kind` is `'fg'`, `'int'` or `'two'`;
  - the gain is 20 or more;
  - `down === 4` on any kind but `'punt'` or `'fg'`;
  - `down > 0 && quarter >= 4 && clock <= 120 && |margin| <= 8`.
- **Extended**, when not Key: `down === 3`, `down > 0 && yardLine >= 80`, `kind === 'sack'`, a gain of 15 or more,
  or `down > 0 && quarter === 2 && clock <= 120`.
- **Full:** everything else, including entries with no `kind`.

- **Anything else** (penalty, kneel, spike, no `kind`): as scrimmage.

### Task 7: Formations

**Files:**
- Create: `src/ui/stage/formations.ts`
- Test: `src/ui/stage/stage.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `Cast`, `FieldScheme`, `FIELD_W`, `clampY`.
- Produces: `type Pt = { x: number; y: number }` and
  `lineUp(e: PlayLogEntry, cast: Cast, off: FieldScheme, def: FieldScheme, ballX: number, ballY: number, dir: 1 | -1, rng: Rng): Map<number, Pt>`.
  `dir` is 1 when the offense is home. A spot `(back, across)` from the ball maps to
  `x = ballX - dir * back`, `y = ballY + dir * across`, so the offense's right is `+dir`, as in `buildPath`'s lanes.

**Spots** (yards; `s` is the strength side, `sign(e.lane)` when `|lane| > 0.3`, otherwise a coin from `rng`):

| Who | back / depth | across |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OL | 0.8 | -4, -2, 0, 2, 4 |
| QB | 1 under center (1st or 2nd down, distance ≤ 10, outside both two-minute windows), else 5 | 0 |
| RB | 6.5 under center; 5 in the gun | 0; `-s * 1.5` in the gun |
| TE (first) / TE (second) | 0.8 | `s * 6` / `-s * 6` |
| WR | 1 | outside first: `s * 20`, `-s * 20`; slots at `±10` on the side with fewer receivers |
| DT | 1 | spread at 3-yard gaps, centred on 0 |
| EDGE | 1 | `±5.5` (`±7.5` in `wide9`) |
| LB | 4.5 | 1 → 0; 2 → ±2.5; 3 → -4, 0, 4 |
| CB | 1 when `coverage.man`, else 6 | over the outermost receiver on each side; a third CB over the slot at 5 |
| S | two-high (`coverage?.twoHigh ?? true`): 12 at ±9; single-high: 13 at 0, and 6 at `s * 4` | |

Every `y` is clamped to [3, `FIELD_W` - 3]. Add a seeded jitter of up to ±0.3 yards on each spot. Kicks: line the
kick units up by kind (kickoff: kickers at their 35 spread across the field; punt: the punter 13 back; field goal:

### Task 8: Scripts for the players in the play

**Files:**
- Create: `src/ui/stage/scripts.ts`
- Test: `src/ui/stage/stage.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `buildPath`, `pointAt`, `Seg` (Task 5); `Cast`, `Pt` (Tasks 6 and 7).
- Produces:
  - `type Keyframe = { t: number; x: number; y: number }`, with `t` in seconds from the snap.
  - `ballTrack(segs: Seg[], dt: number): Float32Array`: x, y, h per step.
  - `script(e: PlayLogEntry, cast: Cast, start: Map<number, Pt>, segs: Seg[], dir: 1 | -1, rng: Rng): Map<number, Keyframe[]>`:
    keyframes for the players in the play only (`who`'s ids, plus a tackler the stager picks when `who` has none).

**Timing:** segment times come from `segs[i].dur`. Wherever a scripted player has the ball, his keyframe at that
moment equals the ball's position.

**By kind:**
- **`run`:** the carrier takes the ball where segment 0 ends and runs segment 1 to its end. The tackler, if any,
  starts from his formation spot and meets the end point at the end time. There's no tackler when the text has
  TOUCHDOWN.
- **`pass`, `incomplete`, `int`:**
  - The QB follows the drop segment.
  - The target leaves at the snap and reaches the throw's landing point (segment 1's end) when the ball does.
  - The cover man trails the target by 1.5 yards (0.7 on `incomplete`); on `int` it's the taker who arrives under the
    ball.
  - Then the ball carrier follows segment 2, if there is one, and the tackler meets him at its end.
- **`sack`:** the QB drops; the rusher starts from his spot, passes within 1 yard of `beat`'s spot at about 40% of the
  play, and meets the QB at the end.
- **`scramble`:** the QB drops, then follows segment 1.
- **`kneel` and `spike`:** the QB only.
- **Anything else:** no keyframes. Kicks are covered in Task 12.

**Fallbacks:** when `who.tackle` is missing on a tackled play (`run`, `pass`, `scramble` without TOUCHDOWN), pick the
defender whose formation spot is nearest the end point. When `who.ball` is missing on `incomplete` (throwaways), pick
no target.

### Task 9: Steering, and the staged play

**Files:**

**Interfaces:**

```ts
export interface StagedPlay {
  dt: number;            // 0.05: 20 steps a second
  steps: number;

  home: boolean[];       // per id: wears the home kit
  pos: Float32Array;     // steps × ids.length × 2: x, y in field yards
  ball: Float32Array;    // steps × 3: x, y, h

  homeId: number; seed: string; snapY: number;

// steer.ts
export function steer(args: {
  cast: Cast; start: Map<number, Pt>; scripted: Map<number, Keyframe[]>; ball: Float32Array;
  thrownAt: number | null; caughtAt: number | null; carrier: number | null; tackler: number | null;
  dir: 1 | -1; dt: number; steps: number; rng: Rng;
}): Float32Array;
```

**Steering**, each step for every unscripted player:
- **Goals by role** (spec §3):
  - OL: get between the nearest rusher and the QB, and hold.
  - DL: push 2 yards, then pursue once the ball is out.
  - Other WR/TE: a decoy route, one of four per spot, seeded: go, out, curl, slant.
  - Zone defenders (when `!coverage.man`): sink to a landmark 8–12 yards deep, then break on the landing point once
    the ball is thrown.
  - Man defenders: mirror their receiver.
  - After the catch or handoff: defenders pursue the ball's spot 0.5 seconds ahead; offensive players move 3 yards
    upfield.
- **Top speeds, yards a second:** CB, S, WR 9; LB, RB 8.5; TE 8; DT, EDGE 7.5; OL 6.5; QB 8.
- **Acceleration:** at most 6 yards a second, per second.
- **Separation:** push apart pairs closer than 1 yard.
- **Wobble:** ±0.2 yards a second, seeded.
- **The tackle rule:** no steered defender may come within 1 yard of the carrier before `tackler` does. Clamp his
  speed toward the carrier as he nears that ring.

`stagePlay` builds the cast, line-up, ball path, scripts and steering, and returns the tracks. It must never throw for
any entry: a kind it can't stage returns the line-up held still for 0.5 seconds.
